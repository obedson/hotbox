# 1. PRODUCT IDENTITY AND MISSION

Product name: Hotbox.

Product category: Postcode-driven community parcel logistics, delivery coordination, verified neighborhood hubs, and workforce enablement.

Initial market: Nigeria.

Core mission:

Use Nigeria's National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System to improve address adoption, establish verified neighborhood parcel hubs, create income opportunities for small business owners and individual riders, and make parcel custody secure and traceable.

Hotbox must make the national postcode useful in people's everyday activities. Customers should use it when booking shipments, verifying destinations, selecting collection points, registering as network partners, and receiving parcels.

The product is simultaneously:

- A postcode-adoption platform.
- A community parcel collection network.
- A courier coordination platform.
- A digital chain-of-custody system.
- A job and earning platform for eligible riders and local businesses.
- A partner verification and compliance platform.
- An enterprise logistics integration platform.

Do not reduce Hotbox to a simple parcel tracking application.

## 1.1 Core business objectives

1. Encourage Nigerians to obtain, understand, and use their official building-level digital postcodes.
2. Improve address resolution for residential and commercial locations.
3. Enable small shop owners to earn income by operating approved parcel hubs.
4. Enable eligible individual riders to access legitimate delivery work.
5. Verify partner identity and residential or business addresses through appropriate authorized processes.
6. Reduce failed deliveries and repeat delivery costs.
7. Improve parcel security through accountable custody transfers.
8. Create a commercially sustainable network.
9. Support licensed courier companies and enterprise shippers.
10. Expand to additional LGAs, states, and regions through a configurable architecture.

## 1.2 Product principles

- A postcode identifies a location; it does not independently prove identity.
- Identity verification and address verification are separate processes.
- Official NIPOST verification, regulatory licensing, and Hotbox Hub accreditation are separate records.
- No partner may claim an official government endorsement that has not been granted.
- No parcel may be released without valid authorization.
- No parcel may have conflicting authoritative custodians.
- No booking may exceed available capacity.
- No financial transaction may be accepted solely because a browser displays a success page.
- No privileged administrative action may occur without authorization and audit logging.
- No external integration may be represented as active until it has been configured and tested.
- Job allocation and partner earnings must be transparent and auditable.
- Security and privacy must be designed into every module.

# 5. POSTCODE-FIRST ADDRESS INFRASTRUCTURE

This is a core feature, not an optional convenience.

Integrate Nigeria's National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode System using an officially authorized integration route.

Official reference:
https://postcode.gov.ng/

Official format standard:
Use Nigeria's National Digital Alphanumeric Postcode (NDAPS) 5-segment hierarchical format, e.g. `FC-01-A03-FK-01` (State-LGA-District-Area-Unit), superseding legacy 6-digit numeric codes (such as 900211). All customer bookings, partner verification, hub addresses, and tracking codes must reference this alphanumeric standard.

Use the official organization/developer access process and comply with the current API terms, usage restrictions, authentication requirements, and data-retention conditions.

Do not scrape the postcode service, invent API endpoints, fabricate postcode records, or assume that access to the website means unrestricted API access.

## 5.1 Address verification workflow

The platform must support:

1. User enters an official postcode.
2. Backend validates the format.
3. Backend calls the configured postcode provider.
4. Provider response is authenticated and validated.
5. The platform retrieves the official location data the integration is authorized to return.
6. The user confirms the intended address.
7. The system stores the verified postcode reference and appropriate verification evidence.
8. The system records provider, verification timestamp, result, and any relevant reference.
9. If verification fails, the system explains the issue and provides an approved recovery path.

Where supported and authorized, the system must support postcode lookup from a building location and address resolution from a postcode.

The platform must not generate an official postcode itself.

## 5.2 Address data model

Support:

- Official postcode.
- Address type.
- State.
- LGA.
- Area or neighborhood.
- Street and building information where available.
- Coordinates.
- Landmark.
- Address source.
- Verification status.
- Verification timestamp.
- Provider reference.
- User-confirmed details.
- Previous address versions where necessary for auditability.

Do not store the full external provider response indiscriminately. Persist only fields permitted by the provider's terms and needed for the product.

## 5.3 Postcode adoption features

Implement a dedicated postcode adoption experience.

Features:

- Explain what a digital postcode is.
- Direct users to the official postcode service.
- Let users look up and confirm their postcode through authorized integration.
- Allow users to save their verified residential and business locations.
- Allow users to share their postcode with explicit consent.
- Include postcode fields in customer bookings.
- Include postcode fields in partner registration.
- Include postcode fields in courier dispatch.
- Include postcode-based hub search.
- Include postcode validation in address changes.
- Provide a postcode adoption dashboard for network administrators.
- Measure postcode usage across bookings, verified partners, active hubs, and service areas.
- Identify locations where adoption is low without publicly exposing private residential data.

Do not imply that Hotbox issues or owns the national postcode.

## 5.4 Address quality and exception handling

A postcode is a location reference, not an identity credential.

Support the following distinct outcomes:

- Format invalid.
- Postcode not found.
- Provider unavailable.
- Address returned but not confirmed.
- Address confirmed by the user.
- Address verified by an authorized provider.
- Manual review required.
- Address verification expired or requires refresh.

Do not silently convert an unavailable external verification service into a successful verification.

## 5.5 Postcode provider adapter

Implement an interface such as:

- `lookupPostcode()`
- `verifyBuildingAddress()`
- `lookupBuildingPostcode()`, if supported.
- `getVerificationStatus()`, if supported.
- `healthCheck()`

These are internal application methods. Map them to actual provider capabilities only after inspecting official documentation.

The adapter must handle authentication, timeouts, rate limits, provider errors, retry policies, response validation, audit logging, and credential rotation.

Use separate sandbox and production configuration.

# 7. THREE DISTINCT APPROVAL SYSTEMS

Implement three independent approval tracks.

## 7.1 Track A: Official postcode/address verification

This establishes the address reference and verification outcome permitted by the authorized postcode provider.

A successful result does not independently verify a person's identity or grant courier operating permission.

## 7.2 Track B: Regulatory licensing and statutory compliance

Maintain official records for the relevant licensed entity, operating scope, licence category, issuer, reference, effective date, expiry, and status.

NIPOST's Courier & Logistics Regulatory Department publishes licensing requirements for courier and logistics operators.

Official reference:
https://clrd.nipost.gov.ng/courier-license

The platform must not assume that Hotbox accreditation substitutes for a statutory licence.

Do not automatically conclude that every individual rider must personally obtain the same licence as a courier company. Determine the actual obligations of Hotbox, partner operators, and individual participants from applicable law and written regulatory clarification.

Before enabling a production service that requires an operator licence, enforce the corresponding compliance gate.

If a required regulatory arrangement is unresolved, allow permitted application and administrative preparation but do not activate the affected regulated service.

## 7.3 Track C: Hotbox accreditation

Hotbox may create its own partner quality and operational accreditation scheme.

Accreditation may consider:

- Identity and address verification.
- Business and site suitability.
- Parcel storage conditions.
- Training.
- Background and risk checks where lawful.
- Service-area coverage.
- Operating hours.
- Security measures.
- Performance history.
- Customer complaints.
- Incident records.
- Partner agreement.
- Commercial terms.

Accreditation is a private network status, not a government licence.

Do not display the NIPOST logo, official government seal, or wording such as "NIPOST certified" unless the exact use and claim are authorized.

Use separate user-interface labels, records, status transitions, and expiry rules for all three tracks.

# 11. SHIPMENT STATE MACHINE AND CUSTODY LEDGER

Implement an explicit server-controlled state machine.

Suggested parcel/shipment states:

- `CREATED`
- `AWAITING_HANDOFF`
- `RESERVED_FOR_PICKUP`
- `COURIER_ASSIGNED`
- `COURIER_PICKUP`
- `IN_TRANSIT`
- `ARRIVED_AT_HUB`
- `READY_FOR_COLLECTION`
- `COLLECTED`
- `DELIVERED`
- `EXCEPTION`
- `RETURN_PENDING`
- `RETURN_IN_TRANSIT`
- `RETURNED`
- `CANCELLED`
- `LOST`
- `DAMAGED`

Define permitted transitions by service type.

Mandatory rules:

1. Every physical custody transfer has a corresponding durable record.
2. Every transfer identifies the previous and new custodian.
3. Every state transition validates the current state.
4. State changes and custody events commit atomically.
5. Terminal states cannot be reopened through normal status updates.
6. Administrative overrides require authorization, a reason, and an audit record.
7. Corrections create additional events instead of deleting historical evidence.
8. Concurrent conflicting transfers must be rejected.
9. Notifications are emitted through a reliable outbox or equivalent mechanism.
10. A failed transaction must not generate a false success notification.

Use database transactions, optimistic locking or row locking, and unique idempotency keys as appropriate.
